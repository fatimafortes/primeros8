-   Operator (Emilio): Narrowed his own opening pitch after tracing it to one real day-one room and finding the civil-protection coordinator has no budget authority, a bespoke building scan costs $25,000 to $200,000, and no Mexican insurer currently ties premiums to drill evidence. Picks Compliance Upgrade, narrowed hard by Operator reality.
-   Adversary (Fatima): Tested eleven kills; three survived sourcing. Kills Compliance Upgrade outright, since CDMX's own brigade training standard pays for hours and topics, not verified performance, so nobody buys proof anyone acted correctly. Weakens Family (the free state drill already owns that slot) and Insurance Bridge (premiums price zone, construction, and insured value, not occupant behavior). Lands on Measurement as the only vacuum she could not close, though her own declared build, an unannounced drill measured with a stopwatch sold to industrial plants, uses no headset and no VR on its own.

## STEP 2. AGREEMENTS

Mexico's drill culture proves reach, not behavior change. Nobody has measured whether the existing national drill format actually changes what people do in a real quake, stated independently by the User and the Adversary, and implicitly by the Operator's own split verdict.

Completion is not competence. Finishing a VR session, a course, or a drill is an attendance fact, not a readiness score; User, Money, and Adversary each say close to this in their own words.

VR hardware and basic action and timing logging are commodity today; behavior transfer to a real earthquake is not. The Meta Quest 3S retails in Mexico at MX$6,600. What is hard and unproven everywhere, not only in Mexico, is showing that a VR decision transfers to real earthquake behavior; the strongest cited study (Feng et al., Auckland) measures knowledge and self efficacy gains, not survival.

No school or family can be the one who proves the model by paying for it first. Money's financing path (private funding, proven results, a lower cost model, broader adoption, a possible public requirement) and the Operator's funding path (CENAPRED, Swiss Re Foundation, or UNAM, never school paid up front) independently land on the same shape.

No current Mexican mechanism rewards or certifies verified rehearsal quality. Not an insurance premium, not a government standard; the CDMX brigade standard itself pays for hours and topics, not outcomes. That recognition gap is real and unclosed by any single build here.

The 1985 to 2017 death-toll drop is not proof that drills work, on its own. Independent sources credit post-1985 building code revisions as the heavier documented factor, with drills and the post-1985 civil protection system as real but secondary contributors. Nobody at the table gets to claim drills, or VR rehearsal, already have that same weight of evidence behind them.

## STEP 3. COLLISIONS

### Collision 1. The first real test population: brigade, campus pilot, or industrial plant workers

The Operator's brigade is the only population with a concrete day-one room, a concrete budget reality, and a funding path already stress-tested against real pressure (CENAPRED, Swiss Re Foundation, UNAM). The User's adult-campus design is not really a rival population; it is a rigorous measurement method, facilitated non-VR versus VR, matched objectives, rubric-scored decisions, follow-up assessment, that was never paired with an institution, a funder, or a legally defined group. The Adversary's industrial workers are a genuinely different market: no headset, no VR, a stopwatch and an unannounced trigger sold as a layer on existing mustering software to multinational plants.
-   Classification: BET. Not resolvable by a source check; a real disagreement about which population and product actually gets built first.
-   Resolved: the school brigade is the team's lead test population for the primary vacuum claim. Two reasons carried the room: it is the only option with a real day-one room, budget, and funding path already defended under pressure, and it structurally sidesteps the Adversary's own age-related kill, since Meta bars accounts under ten outright and requires a parent-managed account for ages ten to twelve, which a brigade of staff and older students avoids by design. The User's measurement design is adopted as the method used to test that brigade, not as a competing population. The Adversary's industrial workers are preserved as the strongest rival bet, the team's own deliberate stress test of whether Measurement survives outside a school and outside VR entirely.

### Collision 2. Does this need to be VR at all?
 The Adversary's own most developed build uses no headset: a surprise trigger and a stopwatch, measuring each worker's protective action in the first seconds. She states directly that this measures more than a headset does. Everyone else assumes VR, or at least simulation, is part of the build.
-   Classification: FACT CHECKABLE against a course constraint, not a live argument. This week's ship requirements state the stack floor plainly: simulation, 3D, or VR, WebXR or a labeled video-sim is acceptable, plus adaptive or ML logic, plus one more signal such as vision, voice, or geodata, and the combination must be multiplicative, not additive.
-   Resolved: a stopwatch and surprise trigger build with no simulation or VR component does not, by itself, meet this week's required stack. The Adversary keeps her core measurement mechanism, the unannounced trigger and the stopwatch scored protective action, but her individual build adds a brief simulated or VR triggered alarm moment that fires the unannounced cue, so the submission sits inside this week's actual floor rather than outside it. This does not weaken her argument that the measurement itself matters more than the headset; it only changes what wraps around it.

### Collision 3. Is the provocation true?
 Money partly agrees, rejecting mass participation alone but not fully rejecting that one strong experience can matter more. The Operator gives a split verdict, agreeing that rote, announced, same- hallway drills do not build instinct, but disagreeing with the literal scale claim as unevidenced. The User flatly rejects the literal claim. The Adversary rules the whole provocation false on both halves, and raised a sharp, specific, checkable question: did the drop in deaths from 1985 to 2017 come from drills, or from the building codes written after 1985?
-   Checked: independent sources credit post-1985 building-code revisions, in 1987, 2004, and 2022, as the heavier documented driver of the reduced death toll and collapse count, alongside new institutions built after 1985 and earthquake drills and public-awareness campaigns as real but secondary contributors. Neither source isolates drills alone as the cause, and neither supports codes alone either. The Adversary's skepticism is partially vindicated: the evidence does not let anyone claim rehearsal, of any kind, already carries the weight of proof that ended Mexico's worst earthquake death tolls. That burden still sits on Measurement to earn.

## STEP 4. THE VACUUM VERDICT

Compliance Upgrade is killed as originally scoped; the existing CDMX brigade standard pays for hours and topics, not verified performance, so nobody buys proof of behavior. It survives only in the Operator's narrowed brigade version, which does not claim to satisfy the existing standard, only to beat it while a real certification path is pursued. Family is being actively entered by the free state incumbent, the Saturday drill, regional scenarios, phone alerts, a free certificate, and nobody built a funded answer to the family specific gap this week. Insurance Bridge is killed as a standalone vacuum, since premiums price zone, construction, and insured value, not occupant behavior; it survives only as a financing layer on top of Measurement, matching Money's own restated position. Measurement survives, and survives strongest, reached independently from all four lenses. Global precedent is real, and was checked, not assumed: Strivr runs on roughly 17,000 headsets across Walmart's US stores, with Walmart reporting 10 to 15 percent higher test scores, a knowledge gain, not a proven behavior-transfer or survival result. Oxford Medical Simulation and the Auckland City Hospital study both prove the standardized, measured-scenario model works elsewhere, again on knowledge and self-efficacy endpoints, not lives saved. Nobody has translated that model into a Mexican earthquake-rehearsal product yet. The play is adapt and localize, not invent from nothing, and every source above is honest that it proves the model is measurable, not that it saves lives.

**PRIMARY VACUUM: Measurement.** Lead test population: the school brigade, tested with the User's

facilitated non-VR vs. VR, rubric scored, follow up assessed method. Rival bet, preserved: the Adversary's industrial-plant, stopwatch-measured build, now carrying a brief simulated or VR trigger to satisfy this week's stack.

## STEP 5. THE CONDITIONS

1. SHADOW CLAUSE: no scenario ever reconstructs a real tragedy, a real location that suffered real deaths, or any real, identifiable victim; every scenario is an original, physically plausible construction only. This is the direct design answer to the chapter's harm, a synthetic experience built on top of a real, specific wound. 2. Every participant gets a private, non-public check for prior earthquake or collapse trauma, an intensity preview, a visible one-tap exit, and a fully legitimate non-VR alternative that is never treated as the lesser option. (The Operator's re-traumatization safeguard and the Adversary's opt-in trauma pre-check converge here independently.) 3. No individual biometric, facial, or emotional capture. Only the decision, action, and timing data the rubric needs is logged; retention is time-boxed and deletable on request; no individually identifiable score ever reaches an employer, insurer, or school administrator, aggregate results only. (The User's retention and no-leaderboard rule and the Adversary's no-biometric-capture rule converge here independently.) 4. Completion is never marketed or treated as certified competence, legal compliance, or proof of survival. Every session's real output is a measured rubric result plus a debrief, and, per Money, a required follow-up physical-drill comparison before any improvement claim is made. 5. Any financial model funds the first proof phase through institutional, grant, or insurer money, never a payment the target population is asked to make before the model is proven. (Money's financing path and the Operator's funding path, checked against real CENAPRED and Swiss Re Foundation precedent, converge here.) 6. The product states plainly, on screen, which specific claims are unverified today, for example that VR training transfers to real-earthquake survival, rather than implying more certainty than the evidence supports.
